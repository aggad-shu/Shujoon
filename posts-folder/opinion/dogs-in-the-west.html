Dogs in the West

Hamza Al-Aqqad – London – 16/11/2026

I held resentment for dogs. I still do. I guess at times it annoys me how much people love dogs. It’s less a distaste for dogs (I do love playing with a dog) and more a fundamental issue with how they are seen and treated, especially in the west.

I dislike when dogs are babied. I dislike when anything but a baby is babied. Being too careful with an expensive product and setting it down gently; am I paying to stress about this thing? I hate seeing adults babied.

I digress.

A psychologist may say I am jealous of dogs. Look at this drawing Edward Hopper did to represent his jealousy of his wife’s cat. I relate to this on a deep level.

Another psychologist may dig deep into why I am enraged by people finding the simple joy of loving a dog. I struggle to accept the idea that a dog can love you. Maybe that says I cannot accept the idea of unconditional love and so I reject dogs as more than animals with a job.

At the same time, people in the west don’t really deal with stray dogs. Man they don’t understand that dogs can be violent. Not all dogs want to love you. These Canaan dogs don’t mess about. Although I do think they get too much hate in Jordan; these dogs are ancient, 9,000 years old.

My dad once bought us a 20JD (20 GBP or so) dog from downtown Amman. That puppy came in a plastic bag and was violent. It bit him. I don’t know what happened to it.

Dogs are animals, and they are great ones. They have a lot of energy and love to run around and jump and get smacked and they collide with stuff and they fall down and they do not feel anything. They are built to be outdoors. Why are we clothing them? Why worry when they fall? Why are we feeding them special foods. It used to be that a ‘doggy bag’ was whatever leftovers that the dog was going to eat. Now they can’t eat anything.

I’m a sucker for those videos of dogs eating raw liver and bones and rabbit ear and chicken hearts. You are an animal, munch on that shit. I dislike the offended looks I get here when I tell people my dog back home in Amman lives outside. There are literal people that live outside. We step over them on our way to work, but a dog living outside is offensive. But a homeless person with a dog, then people stop.

This is starting to feel like how Tony Soprano dreaming about ducks taking his penis is actually his fear of his family abandoning him. I’ll stop there.
