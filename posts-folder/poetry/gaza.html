Choose, Oh Son of Gaza

Hamza Al-Aqqad – 11/9/2026 – London

A poem Dedicated to Yahya Mahmoud, with a backdrop of clips from a movie filmed in Gaza, Tale of the Three Jewels.

Choose, oh son of Gaza
The prison cell or the drone
For you will find that all roads lead to Gaza

You will find that the difference is merely a letter
Like the difference between freedom and love
Or love and war
Or war and freedom

Your voice is heard, son of gaza
We will never forget you
For even in our hearts, and behind our eyelids
All roads lead to Gaza
